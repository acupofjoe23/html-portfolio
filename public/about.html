About Me

I have worked as a software engineer for nearly six years (as of April 2026). I began my career in electronics after earning a bachelor’s degree in electrical engineering, where I documented user workflows and ran simulations against datasets in an RF sensor lab.

Early on I was assigned database work in SQL Server Management Studio, which introduced me to structured problem-solving in software. When I could either transition into a software role or return to documentation and testing, I chose software. The role demanded depth I had not yet developed; over the following three years I built the foundation I still rely on today.

Moving from a web-service project to a standalone microservice architecture is where I gained real momentum as an engineer. I work comfortably in Java, C#, and SQL, and I am deepening my skills in C++, CSS, HTML, and JavaScript, including React-based front ends. Recently I have focused on web applications because the discipline has piqued my interest and aligns with where I want to grow professionally.
