The Best Movies According to Joe

My Top 3 Movies of All-Time

RobinHood Men In Tights: Comedy

This is comedy spinnoff of the classic Robinhood story. The story takes place after Robin returns from fighting in King Richard's Crusades.
After returning he finds that all of his family has passed and his lands are being reclaimed by the King's brother. The story follows
the wildly silly events that occur as he attempts to get his lands back. Love this movie, it never fails to get a laugh from me.

Edge Of Tomorrow: Sci-Fi

There is a lot that can be said about this movie. It follows the paradigm of moveis where the main character is caught in a loop of the same day
except in this story the character is faced with the monumentous task of saving the world. I won't go into too much detail but this is definitely
a must watch for any sci-fi lover.

All 3 Venom Movies: Super Hero Movie

This is one of those super hero movies that just seem to flow naturally.... or atleast as natural as a person with super powers can be. it really captures
how I think a person in real life would react to having an alien symbiote that grants them super human abilities when transformed. Love this story
It's a nice break from the repetitive story line of many super hero movies we've been seeing for the past several years.
